AUSTRALIAN STUDENT & POST-STUDY VISA COSTS — REFERENCE GUIDE
=============================================================
Last updated: May 2026
Sources: Department of Home Affairs (immi.homeaffairs.gov.au), 
ICEF Monitor reporting (March 2026), Study Australia.

Always verify current fees with the Department of Home Affairs
Charge Estimator before applying, as visa application charges
are reviewed annually (usually around 1 July) and can change
mid-year in response to policy decisions.


1. STUDENT VISA (SUBCLASS 500) — APPLICATION CHARGE
---------------------------------------------------
Primary applicant base charge: AUD $2,000
- This is the main visa application fee paid via ImmiAccount
  at the time of lodgement.
- It is non-refundable, even if the application is refused.
- This rate has been in effect since 1 July 2025, when it was
  increased from AUD $1,600. Before that it was AUD $710.

Secondary applicants:
- Spouse/de facto partner and dependent children added to the
  application each pay an additional charge.
- Verify the exact secondary applicant charge using the Visa
  Pricing Estimator on the Department of Home Affairs website,
  as these are reviewed periodically.

Subsequent Temporary Application Charge (STAC):
- May apply if you are applying onshore while already holding
  a substantive visa. Check the official charge estimator.


2. STUDENT VISA — FINANCIAL CAPACITY REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------
Living cost thresholds (2026, used to demonstrate financial
capacity, not paid as a fee):
- Single student: AUD $29,710 per year
- Spouse / de facto partner: additional AUD $10,394 per year
- Each dependent child: additional AUD $4,449 per year
- School-aged children (5–18): an additional AUD $13,502 per
  child per year for schooling costs

Plus:
- Tuition fees for the first 12 months of your course (as
  shown on your CoE)
- Return travel costs: typically AUD $1,000–$2,500 per person


3. ADDITIONAL MANDATORY OR LIKELY COSTS
----------------------------------------
Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC):
- Mandatory for the entire duration of your visa.
- Cost varies by provider, policy type, and family size.
- Premium increases of around +4.4% were announced for
  April 2026.

English language test:
- IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, Cambridge, or OET.
- Test fees typically AUD $300–$400 depending on the provider.
- Required unless you qualify for an exemption (e.g., passport
  from UK, USA, Canada, NZ, or Republic of Ireland; or 5+
  years of study in English).

Health examination:
- Required for some applicants depending on country of origin
  and length of stay.
- Paid directly to the panel physician, typically AUD $300–$500.

Biometrics collection:
- Required at a Visa Application Centre (VAC) for some
  applicants. Cost varies by country.

Police clearance / character checks:
- May be required from each country you have lived in for
  12+ months in the past 10 years. Costs vary by country.

Document translation and certification:
- NAATI-accredited translation required for non-English
  documents. Costs vary.


4. TEMPORARY GRADUATE VISA (SUBCLASS 485) — APPLICATION CHARGE
---------------------------------------------------------------
This is the main post-study work visa for international
graduates and is the most relevant pathway after a 500.

Primary applicant base charge: AUD $4,600
- This rate took effect on 1 March 2026.
- It was AUD $2,300 between July 2025 and February 2026.
- Before that it was approximately AUD $1,895 (July 2024).
- Across three increases in just over a year, the application
  charge has more than doubled.

Secondary applicants:
- Partner / dependent aged 18 or over: AUD $2,300
- Dependent child under 18: AUD $1,150

Stay duration on the 485 (Post-Higher Education Work stream):
- Bachelor's degree: 2 years
- Master's by coursework: 2 years
- Master's by research: 3 years
- Doctoral (PhD): 3 years

Stay duration on the 485 (Post-Vocational Education Work
stream): typically 18 months for eligible VET graduates in
occupations on the relevant skilled list.


5. WHY FEES KEEP CHANGING — CONTEXT
------------------------------------
The Australian government has framed recent visa fee
increases as part of a broader strategy to manage international
student numbers and weed out non-genuine applicants. The
2025 student visa fee increase from $1,600 to $2,000 was
projected to generate around AUD $760 million over four
years, intended to fund migration system improvements and
regional infrastructure. Australia's Subclass 500 application
fee is currently the most expensive student visa fee among
major English-speaking destinations.


6. PRACTICAL BUDGETING TIPS
----------------------------
- Build a 5–10% contingency buffer into your study-abroad
  budget to absorb fee increases or currency fluctuations.
- Confirm fees through the official Charge Estimator before
  paying, never rely on third-party blog posts (including
  this one) as the final source of truth.
- Apply within Australia (onshore) only after checking
  current eligibility — restrictions on visa hopping from
  some temporary visas tightened from July 2025.
- The visa application charge is non-refundable regardless
  of whether your visa is granted, so prepare a strong
  application before paying.
