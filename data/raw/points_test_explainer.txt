GENERAL SKILLED MIGRATION POINTS TEST — EXPLAINER
==================================================
Last updated: May 2026
Sources: Department of Home Affairs (immi.homeaffairs.gov.au),
SkillSelect points table.

This file explains how points are calculated for the
points-tested skilled visas: subclass 189 (Skilled
Independent), subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated), and
subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional).

You must score at least 65 points to submit an Expression
of Interest (EOI). However, the minimum to be COMPETITIVE
and actually receive an invitation is much higher — typically
85-95+ points for the 189 in 2025-26, depending on occupation.


WHEN POINTS ARE LOCKED IN
--------------------------
Your points are assessed at TWO moments:
1. When you submit your EOI in SkillSelect.
2. At the date of invitation (Invitation to Apply / ITA).

If you turn a higher age band, your wedding date passes,
your test score expires, or your skills assessment lapses
BEFORE the ITA, your points change. Many invitations are
withdrawn at the verification stage for this reason.


CATEGORY 1: AGE
----------------
Maximum 30 points. Age is calculated at the time of invitation.
- 18-24:   25 points
- 25-32:   30 points
- 33-39:   25 points
- 40-44:   15 points
- 45+:     Ineligible — you must be UNDER 45 at invitation.

Implication: Many applicants try to time their EOI to capture
the highest possible age points before aging out of a band.


CATEGORY 2: ENGLISH LANGUAGE
-----------------------------
Maximum 20 points. Based on your most recent English test
result (valid for 3 years).
- Competent English:  0 points (required minimum — IELTS 6.0
                      in each band, or equivalent)
- Proficient English: 10 points (IELTS 7.0 in each band,
                      or equivalent)
- Superior English:   20 points (IELTS 8.0 in each band,
                      or equivalent)

Accepted tests include IELTS Academic, IELTS General Training,
PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, OET, and Cambridge English.

This is one of the most controllable point categories. Many
applicants retake tests multiple times to push from 10 to 20.


CATEGORY 3: SKILLED EMPLOYMENT OVERSEAS
----------------------------------------
Maximum 15 points. Must be in your nominated occupation or
a closely related occupation. Skills assessing authority
must confirm the work counts.
- Less than 3 years:   0 points
- 3-4 years:           5 points
- 5-7 years:          10 points
- 8-10 years:         15 points


CATEGORY 4: SKILLED EMPLOYMENT IN AUSTRALIA
--------------------------------------------
Maximum 20 points. Combined with overseas experience, the
maximum across both categories is 20 points.
- Less than 1 year:    0 points
- 1-2 years:           5 points
- 3-4 years:          10 points
- 5-7 years:          15 points
- 8-10 years:         20 points

Implication: Australian work experience is worth substantially
more per year than overseas experience. This is a major reason
to use 485 work rights aggressively to build points.


CATEGORY 5: EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATIONS
---------------------------------------
Maximum 20 points. Highest qualification only — these do not
stack.
- Doctorate (PhD) from Australian or recognised institution:
  20 points
- Bachelor's degree (including Bachelor's Honours) or Master's:
  15 points
- Diploma or trade qualification: 10 points
- Award/qualification recognised by the assessing authority
  but not above: 10 points


CATEGORY 6: AUSTRALIAN STUDY REQUIREMENT
-----------------------------------------
5 points. Awarded if you completed at least one degree,
diploma, or trade qualification from an Australian institution
that:
- Took at least 2 academic years (92 weeks of study).
- Was completed in person in Australia.
- Was taught in English.

This is the easiest 5 points to earn for any international
student who completed a 2-year+ Australian qualification.


CATEGORY 7: SPECIALIST EDUCATION QUALIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------
10 points. Awarded for a Master's by research or PhD from an
Australian institution where the qualification includes at
least 2 years of study in one of the following:
- Biological sciences
- Computer sciences
- Engineering and related technologies
- Mathematical sciences
- Natural and physical sciences

(Often called "STEM points" informally.)


CATEGORY 8: STUDY IN REGIONAL AUSTRALIA
----------------------------------------
5 points. Awarded if your Australian Study Requirement
(2-year qualification) was completed at a campus in a
designated regional area.

This does NOT include Sydney, Melbourne, or Brisbane
metropolitan areas.


CATEGORY 9: ACCREDITED COMMUNITY LANGUAGE
------------------------------------------
5 points. Awarded if you are accredited at the paraprofessional
level (or above) by NAATI in a recognised community language
other than English.


CATEGORY 10: PARTNER SKILLS
----------------------------
Up to 10 points.
- Partner meets age, skills, English, and occupation
  requirements: 10 points
- Partner has Competent English only: 5 points
- Single applicant, OR partner is Australian citizen/PR:
  10 points (yes, being single can earn the same as a
  fully-skilled partner)


CATEGORY 11: PROFESSIONAL YEAR
-------------------------------
5 points. Awarded for completing a Professional Year
program (typically 12 months) in accounting, IT, or
engineering through an approved provider.

Often pursued by recent graduates on 485 visas to:
- Earn 5 extra points.
- Gain practical Australian work experience.
- Strengthen their employability.


CATEGORY 12: STATE/TERRITORY NOMINATION OR FAMILY SPONSORSHIP
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Subclass 190 (state-nominated): 5 points
- Subclass 491 (state-nominated OR eligible family-sponsored): 15 points

These points only count if you are applying for the
corresponding visa.


WORKED EXAMPLE — TYPICAL RECENT GRADUATE
-----------------------------------------
Profile: 28 years old, Master's degree from an Australian
non-regional university, Proficient English (IELTS 7+),
2 years of Australian work experience, no Professional Year,
single, no STEM specialist points.

- Age (25-32): 30
- Proficient English: 10
- Master's degree: 15
- Australian Study Requirement: 5
- Australian work experience (2 years): 5
- Single: 10

Total: 75 points (below the 85-95+ competitive threshold
for 189, but viable for a state-nominated 190 with +5 = 80,
or a regional 491 with +15 = 90).


WHERE TO IMPROVE A WEAK SCORE
------------------------------
Most students reach this calculation and realise they need
more points. The most controllable improvements are:
- Pushing English from Proficient (10) to Superior (20):
  +10 points. Often the single biggest unlock.
- More Australian work experience: 5 → 10 → 15 → 20 points
  over time.
- Professional Year program: +5 points.
- NAATI community language accreditation: +5 points.
- State nomination: +5 (190) or +15 (491).
- Studying in regional Australia: +5 points (but this is
  a decision made years before applying).


SUBMIT AN EOI BEFORE YOU "FEEL READY"
--------------------------------------
The EOI is free and easy to update. Submit early with your
current honest score, then revise as your situation improves.
Your EOI's lodge date can affect tie-breaking in invitation
rounds, so earlier is better — even if you start below the
competitive threshold.


THIS IS A GENERAL EXPLAINER — NOT MIGRATION ADVICE
---------------------------------------------------
Points rules change. Edge cases exist. For decisions about
your specific case, consult a MARA-registered migration agent
or use the official SkillSelect points calculator on the
Department of Home Affairs website.
