Family members and your Student visa

Studying in Australia can be an exciting experience for you and your family. If you plan to bring family members with you, it is important to understand the Student visa requirements before you apply.

Only eligible applicants can include family members in a Student visa application. Depending on your circumstances, this may include applicants who:

are studying a PhD degree
are sponsored by the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade or Defence
receive a fully-funded foreign government scholarship
are eligible students from a Pacific country or an ASEAN member country.

If an exemption applies, family members you may be able to include are:

your partner (spouse or de facto partner)
your dependent children
your partner's dependent children.

Important things to know:

If you are eligible to include family members, everyone included in the application must be in the same location when you apply.
Student visa holders cannot add family members after their Student visa has been granted.
Bringing family members can affect your study plans, accommodation and budget. Understand the requirements before making study, travel or relocation plans.

Frequently asked questions
What should I know about bringing children to Australia?

If your children are included in your Student visa application, research schooling and childcare options before you arrive. School starting ages, enrolment arrangements and costs can vary between Australian states and territories. If you have younger children, you may also need to arrange childcare or kindergarten. Some services have waiting lists, so explore your options early.

Learn more about Schooling options in Australia.

What if I am under 18?

If you are under 18, a parent, custodian or eligible relative may be able to come to Australia to care for and support you.

They may need to apply for a Student Guardian visa and meet the relevant requirements. A Student Guardian can provide accommodation, welfare and other support while you study in Australia.

If your welfare arrangements change while you are under 18, you must notify the Department of Home Affairs (DHA).

Learn about the Student Guardian visa.

What should I consider before moving my family to Australia?

Moving overseas with your family requires careful planning. Before you apply:

Confirm whether family members can be included in your application
Understand the current Student visa requirements
Review the visa conditions that may apply to you and your family members
Research education and childcare arrangements
Plan your family’s living and financial costs in Australia.

Planning early can help you and your family prepare for living in Australia.
